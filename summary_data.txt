What data to include in the summary:
    - final score
    - player final points and player projected points
    - records before and after
    - how the team performed relative to projection
    - where the teams land in the standings now, and their records before and after
    - how the teams performed relative to rest of league (determines whether it is a slugfest or a slog)
    - a way to capture how the matchup progressed (needs to be digestible by the llm generating summary)
        - every instance of points being scored (called an "activity" for these purposes), stored sequentially. So Patrick Mahomes might score 4 points
        followed by James Cook scoring 2, followed by etc. etc.
        - if a player in the matchup has consecutive activities with no other players with an activity in between,
        these activities will be combined into one by summing the scores
            - the only exception to this is if a player's consecutive activities have different signed point values (one positive one negative)
        - each activity should have the following stored: 
            - the player involved in the activity
            - the point value associated with the activity
            - which team the activity scored for
            - the matchup's score following the activity
            - if the activity resulted in a lead change
            - what time and day of the week the activity took place
            - which NFL slot the game took place in (TNF, Sunday noon, Sunday afternoon, Sunday night, Monday night, other - like Sunday morning london or something)
        - ideally, this will allow the LLM to identify key moments in the matchup, or identify unique parts of the matchup (lots of lead changes, complete dominance by one team, a team being carried by a player)
    - managerial decisions pre-matchup (or mid-matchup):
        - if either of the teams started someone injured or on bye
        - what waiver / free agent decisions they made
        - if they switched anybody on or off their roster during the matchup
